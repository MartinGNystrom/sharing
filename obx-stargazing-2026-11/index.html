03Night sky timeline

Computed for North Core Banks (34.98 N, 76.20 W). Sun and Moon times cross-checked with USNO. The flat ocean horizon to the east and south is the main asset.

Night 1

Fri 2026-11-06 into Sat 11-07
  1. 17:32Civil twilight ends. Saturn already up in the ESE.
  2. 18:31MILKY WAYSummer Milky Way arches overhead through Cygnus (Deneb near the zenith) down to the SW. Look low SW first; the galactic center sets about 19:20.
  3. 19:00M31Andromeda Galaxy high in the NE to E, about 50° up at dark, highest ~21:45. Naked eye in a dark sky, better in 10x50s. Double Cluster in Perseus nearby.
  4. 20:00PLEIADESPleiades climbing in the ENE. Highest ~00:50.
  5. 20:35ORIONOrion starts rising in the E; well up after 23:00, highest ~02:35. Sirius rises ~22:30.
  6. 21:36SATURNSaturn highest in the S, ~56° up, in Cetus, mag ~0.6. Sets 03:41.
  7. all nightTAURIDSSouthern Taurid fireball peak was 11-04/05, Northern peak 11-11/12. Low rates, but known for bright fireballs. Best after midnight when Taurus is high.
  8. 23:48Mars rises, then Jupiter at 00:06 (mag -1.9), both in Leo, E.
  9. 04:53Venus rises; the 3% crescent Moon rises 04:56.
  10. 05:05Astronomical dawn.
  11. 05:45VENUS + MOONCrescent Moon ~1.5° from Venus, Spica ~1.3° from Venus, all ~9° up, ESE. Binoculars.

Sat 2026-11-07 into Sun 11-08

  2. 18:31MILKY WAYSame arc as Night 1. Moon does not rise until 05:58, after dawn begins: the darkest night of the trip.

05Rules on the island

North Core Banks, Cape Lookout National Seashore. Per NPS camping and ORV pages.

Camping and driving

  • Camping is free, no permit per NPS (camping page updated 2026-04-04). 14-day limit. The Morris Marina FAQ says a free permit is required. confirm with rangers
  • Truck-bed camping only on the ocean beach, seaward of the primary dunes. Not on the back road unless signed.
  • ORV permit required for the truck. Show the paper permit to a ranger for the decal. Nov decal pickup spot TBD
  • Night driving is allowed in November.
  • No driving in the Portsmouth historic district.

Fires, water, facilities

  • Fires only below the high-tide line, max 3 ft. Never in Portsmouth Village.
  • No potable water. Cabins, bathhouse, and dump station closed.
  • Portsmouth Village visitor center closed, unstaffed after late October.
  • Pack out all trash.
  • NPS conditions page last updated 2026-04-08, before the September storm. No storm reports found for North Core Banks. ask rangers about beach condition

07Go/no-go

Decide at 72 h (2026-11-03) and 24 h (2026-11-05). Thresholds are suggestions, not park or ferry rules.

Go if all are true

  • Morris Marina confirms both sailings.
  • At least one of Fri or Sat night shows mostly clear skies (roughly 30% cloud or less) after 18:30 in Clear Outside, Astrospheric, and NWS MHX sky cover.
  • No gale warning or small craft advisory for the sound or coastal waters on crossing days.
  • No NPS closure or alert for North Core Banks.
  • Overnight lows manageable with a 30 F bag.

No-go if any is true

  • Ferry cancels or cannot confirm a return sailing.
  • Overcast or rain forecast for both nights.
  • Strong wind or a coastal storm forecast: crossings may cancel and you could be stuck.
  • NPS closes the beach or ORV routes.

If no-go: move to 2026-11-13 to 11-16, or use the Ocracoke fallback if only the ferry is the problem.

Also check DriveNC and NCDOT ferry alerts before leaving.

08Gear checklist

Truck and sand

  • Tire gauge; air down to 15 to 20 psi
  • Compressor to re-inflate
  • Shovel
  • Jack board
  • Soft Sand mode on the island
  • NACS adapter if needed

Sleep and cold

  • Sleeping bag rated 30 F or lower
  • Closed-cell foam under the air mattress
  • Wool layers, hat, gloves
  • Hand warmers
  • Camp Mode pre-heat
  • Rain shell for heavy dew and wind

Sky

  • Red headlamp
  • Stellarium / SkySafari on phone, night mode
  • 10x50 binoculars
  • Reclining chair
  • Tripod
  • Lens dew heater and power

Water and camp

  • 1 gal water per person per day, plus spare
  • Food for 2 nights
  • Trash bags; pack everything out
  • Firewood only if fire planned (below high-tide line)
  • First aid kit

Nov normals ~63/51 F (Ocracoke), colder behind fronts. Expect wind and heavy dew.
